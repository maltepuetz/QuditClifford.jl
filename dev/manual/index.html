On this page

Manual ​

The manual explains the package topic by topic. Getting Started is the faster introduction; the Examples show complete workflows; the API Reference lists every exported function.

Conventions defines the operators, orderings and signs that the rest of the documentation relies on: the Weyl operators and , phase exponents, the symplectic form, and the direction in which gates act. Read it before building raw tableaux or interpreting phase exponents.

Tableaux covers the two tableau types and when to use each, preset and raw construction, the contract the generators satisfy, and copying, comparing and resetting a tableau.

Measurements, Expectations and Channels covers projective Pauli measurements and their outcomes, expectation values, and the channels that discard information: dephasing, trace-out and reset.

Clifford Unitaries lists the named gates with their action on Paulis and on basis states, and covers stored Clifford operators with conjugation, composition and inversion.

Entanglement Measures defines the entropy, the negativity and the mutual, conditional and tripartite information, all as whole numbers of units.

Random Sampling draws uniformly random Clifford operators and stabilizer states, and shows how one seeded RNG reproduces a whole monitored-circuit trajectory.

Inspecting States expands a small tableau into an exact ket, a state vector or a density matrix, for debugging and understanding.

Limitations and Performance states which dimensions are supported and how large d may be, and how to keep simulation loops fast and free of allocation.
